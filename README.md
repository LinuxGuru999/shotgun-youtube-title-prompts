# YouTube Title Prompts

Generate clickable titles without being clickbait

## Overview
YouTube Title Prompts is a digital prompt pack designed for creators who want to increase their Click-Through Rate without resorting to misleading clickbait. It provides structured formulas and AI-ready templates to help you craft compelling, honest titles that accurately represent your content while driving clicks.

## Use cases
- Overcoming writer's block when you have a finished video but no idea what to name it.
- Revamping the title of an underperforming video to test a new angle and revive views.
- Systematizing your brainstorming process so you can generate multiple strong title options in minutes.

## How to use
1. Download the prompt pack instantly as a PDF or notion file.
2. Copy one of the 30 title formulas or curiosity templates.
3. Paste the template into your AI tool of choice (like ChatGPT or Claude) along with your video topic to generate optimized options.

## What is included
- 30 title formulas
- Curiosity templates
- A/B variant prompts
